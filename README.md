# MME 313: *Mechanical Behaviour of Materials*, online companion

This is the online companion to the textbook *Mechanical Behaviour of Materials* (3rd edition) by H. M. Mamun Al Rashed. The book is written for MME 313 in the Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), Dhaka.

The printed book links here, with a QR code, on the first page of Appendix B.

## What is here

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`appendix-B/`](appendix-B/) | **Appendix B, "Derivations Beyond the Course", in full.** The printed book gives a short entry for each derivation: what is derived and the result. The full step-by-step derivations are here. The section, equation and table numbers are the same as in the book, so a pointer in a chapter ("Appendix B.3") finds its place in both. |
| [`colour-figures/`](colour-figures/) | **The figures of Chapters 1 to 8 in colour**, one PDF per chapter, with the numbers and captions they have in the printed book. Every curve in the book has its own line pattern as well as its colour, so a black-and-white print can still be read. The colour versions are here for students who want to see each figure as it was drawn. References in the captions point to the printed book. |

### Which figures are in the colour PDFs

The colour PDFs contain:

- the drawings made for the book;
- the drawings made "after" a textbook figure, which take only the idea from it (the caption names the textbook).

These appear in the printed book only, and each PDF lists their numbers at the end:

- drawings that reproduce a textbook figure ("adapted from" in the caption);
- photographs, micrographs and other image files. They are black and white, so a colour copy would add nothing.
